# Classical models: validation results

_Generated by `scripts/train.py --config configs/classical.yaml` — do not edit by hand. Test-set results are produced separately in the evaluation phase._

Each classifier was tuned with 30 Optuna (TPE) trials maximizing macro F1 on the validation split, then refitted from scratch on the training split.

- *Train time* is a fresh fit of the full pipeline (vectorizers, classifier and, for the SVM, 5-fold probability calibration).
- *Search time* for the first classifier includes building the shared TF-IDF cache; later classifiers reuse it.
